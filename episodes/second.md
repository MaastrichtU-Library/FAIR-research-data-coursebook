::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: objectives

- Recognize when a standard license is sufficient and when a custom agreement is needed.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

Data terms of use are a text statement that explains how a dataset may be
accessed, reused, cited, or restricted. They give future users a practical and
legal basis for reusing a resource.

## What are data terms of use?

Terms of use often combine several pieces of information:

- what resource the statement applies to
- what license or permissions apply
- how attribution should be given
- what limitations or disclaimers should be considered

A short terms-of-use statement is often enough for an openly shared dataset,
while more sensitive or collaborative projects may require a longer agreement.

## Minimum components

At a minimum, a useful statement should answer four questions:

- Description: what dataset or digital object does this apply to?
- License: under what conditions may others reuse it?
- Attribution: how should the resource be cited or acknowledged?
- Disclaimer: what important caveats should a reuser know?

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: callout

### Terms of use support reuse

The goal of a terms-of-use statement is not only legal clarity. It also helps
other people understand whether and how they can confidently reuse the data.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

## Accessible text formats

Terms of use should be stored in formats that are easy to read and preserve,
such as Markdown, plain text, or HTML.

Projects often place these statements in files such as `README`, `LICENSE`, or
separate policy documentation.

## Standard licenses

Two commonly used licensing families for data are:

- [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/about/cclicenses/)
- [Open Data Commons](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/index.html)

Standard licenses are useful when they already match your reuse conditions.
If they do not, a custom terms-of-use statement or data use agreement may be
more appropriate.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: keypoints

- A data terms-of-use statement explains the basis for access and reuse.
- A useful statement should identify the resource, license, attribution, and disclaimers.
- Standard licenses are helpful when they match the intended reuse conditions.
