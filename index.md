# FAIR Research Data Coursebook

This lesson introduces practical ways to apply FAIR research data principles
through concrete data management and reuse tasks.

## Lesson Focus

The coursebook is organized around six recurring FAIR data tasks:

1. Set up your own terms
2. Speak the same language
3. Securely share
4. Publish and preserve
5. Make machines work for you
6. Responsibly reuse
